Repair Conditioner

What it does

Bond-friendly conditioner for colour-treated, heat-styled lengths.

Ingredients

Aqua, Cetearyl Alcohol, Behentrimonium Chloride, Glycerin, Hydrolysed Baobab Protein, Squalene, Argania Spinosa (Argan) Kernel Oil, Amodimethicone, Cetrimonium Chloride, Sodium Cocoyl Glycinate, Panthenol, Citric Acid, Phenoxyethanol, Sodium Benzoate, Parfum, Limonene, Hexyl Cinnamal.
